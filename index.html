What this is

Two hundred dots, moving on their own. In time they will flock, and scatter when your pointer comes near — today they only drift, and the page is published anyway. Publishing early, while there is almost nothing here to go wrong, is one of the things this project is trying to show.

It is a worked example of agent-project-rules, a published ruleset for running projects with AI agents. What the dots may do, what may be drawn, and what a test is allowed to claim were all decided in writing before any of it was built. The documentation is the point; the toy is what the documentation is about.
